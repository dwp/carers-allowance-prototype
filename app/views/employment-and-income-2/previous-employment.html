Earnings from any jobs that have ended in the last 40 days

If you have finished a job within the last 40 days, you need to tell us about it. This may affect your eligibility for Carer's Allowance.

We can get most of the information from Department of Work and Pensions (DWP) and HMRC. We only ask about the details not available.
