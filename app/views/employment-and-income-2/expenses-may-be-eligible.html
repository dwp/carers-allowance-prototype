You may be able to get Carer's Allowance

You told us you earn more than £204 a week. This usually means you would not be eligible.

You could still be eligible if you have certain costs, such as:

  • expenses from your job at
  • paying someone else to care for
  • paying for childcare
  • making contributions into a private pension

If your earnings vary

You may be eligible if some weeks you get more than £204. If this is the case, we will work this out for you if you continue.
