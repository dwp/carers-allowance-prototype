Childcare costs

We will:

  • get the amount you were paid from HMRC
  • work out how much of your childcare costs we can take off your earnings

We can take off up to 50% of your net pay. That is the amount left after taking off tax and National Insurance.

For example, if your net pay is £1,000 a month and you pay £600 in care costs, we could take off up to £500.
